Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

We believe informed patients make better health decisions. Find answers to your most pressing questions about our care, services, and procedures.

Talk to Us →
How do I book an appointment?
+
You can book an appointment through our online booking form on this page, call our 24/7 patient line at +1 (800) 484-8224, or visit us in person. Telehealth consultations are also available and can be booked online in minutes.
What insurance plans does VitaCare accept?
+
We accept all major insurance providers including Aetna, BlueCross BlueShield, Cigna, UnitedHealth, Humana, and Medicare/Medicaid. Our billing team will verify your coverage before your appointment at no charge.
Are telehealth consultations available?
+
Yes, we offer secure video consultations for a wide range of specialties. Telehealth appointments are available 7 days a week, including evenings and weekends, from 7am to 10pm. You'll receive a secure link via email before your session.
How long is a typical wait time?
+
Our average wait time for routine appointments is under 48 hours. For urgent care needs, we offer same-day and next-day appointments. Emergency consultations are handled immediately upon arrival at our facility.
How is my medical data protected?
+
We use enterprise-grade encryption and are fully HIPAA-compliant. Your medical records are stored on secure, audited servers and are never shared with third parties without your explicit consent. We hold ISO 27001 certification for information security.
Do you offer second opinion consultations?
+
Absolutely. We strongly encourage second opinions and offer a dedicated service for reviewing previous diagnoses and treatment plans. Our specialists will provide an independent, thorough assessment within 5 business days of receiving your records.
